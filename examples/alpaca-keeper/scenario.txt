# Example copied from the proto-bovine repo (conversation-maps/alpaca-keeper) on 19 Oct 2026.
# A real map from a Defra programme, kept here to show the tool at scale:
#   - a protagonist declared `once` in groups of two or three threads, so one person is
#     seen pulled into several conversations while institutions repeat per thread
#   - the `lego` sprite template (templates/lego), with `?name` placeholders that resolve
#     to sprites and fall back to dashed boxes if a sprite is missing
#   - a thread-comment convention: status (CURRENT, PROPOSED, ASSUMED, CONTESTED), the
#     assumption IDs it rests on (A-numbers from that project's register), and "Asked:"
#     naming who has been consulted. The "Asked" line is the persuasive layer.
# Names of individuals and suppliers have been generalised; the A-numbers are left as they
# were and mean nothing outside the source project.
#
# Day 1 for an alpaca keeper: the conversations one operator has to have.
# Drafted 14 Oct 2026 for the cross-cutting show and tell. One map per operator; the
# grid rows (registration, identification, record keeping, monitoring) are the acts.
# Companion map: sheep-keeper (a route that exists and changes). This one is the route
# that becomes mandatory on Day 1 for a sector of about 65,000 animals, mostly hobbyists.

# ---- Act 1: the letter ----

# 1. The letter. PROPOSED. A025 agreed in workshop (every camelid establishment registers,
# no exemptions); A027 OPEN (the existing voluntary route simply becomes mandatory).
# 12 Oct: "a reuse piece". Asked: nobody has tested a letter or the route with a keeper.
letter: From Day 1 you must register your alpacas         #day1
keeper: Register with whom? I'm not a farm
letter: Apply for a CPH and a herd mark, like cattle
keeper: I've never heard of a CPH                          #cph

# ---- Act 2: registration ----

# 2. The CPH. PROPOSED. Plain-English Part 3: three CPH front doors kept, RPA online form
# in England with manual entry into SAM. The form was designed for farms.
# Asked: a business analyst on the route. No camelid keeper on the form.
keeper: Six alpacas behind the house. Do I need a CPH?
rpa: Yes. Fill in the form, I'll post your number
keeper: It asks about cattle and crops. Leave them blank?

# 3. The herd mark. ASSUMED. A118 OPEN (the LITP single CPH and herd-mark application, live
# since July 2026, covers or can extend to camelids). 12 Oct: SAM already captures alpacas
# and llamas; a BA has been asked to log in and record which fields appear. Asked: a BA.
keeper: Got my CPH. Now a herd mark?
sam@apha: I've a box for llamas and alpacas
apha: What's in it? Someone log in and look

# 4. The holding. ASSUMED. A030 OPEN (a GB holding maps cleanly to an EU establishment);
# A031 OPEN (facilities, type and category need only minimal data changes once defined).
# Asked: nobody. "Facilities" was still with policy in early October.
paddock: Three acres and a shed. Am I an establishment?
sam: Give me coordinates, capacity, type and facilities
paddock: Facilities? A shelter and a hose

# ---- Act 3: identification ----

# 5. The tag. ASSUMED. A056 CONFIRMED in pack (pre-regime microchips are not official ID,
# ear tags are the alternative); A057 OPEN, must be true (approved device manufacturers
# will exist by Day 1; none approved yet). A026 OPEN (all camelids, not only new births).
# Asked: identification policy and suppliers. No keeper, no vet.
keeper2: They're all microchipped already
apha_b: Those don't count. You need approved tags
keeper2: Where do I buy them?
!apha_b: Nobody's approved yet                              #no-tags

# 6. The number. ASSUMED. A052 OPEN (Scotland and Wales agree 826-18 for camelids);
# A051 OPEN (LUIS lead time fits before 30 June 2027; the LIS supplier takes on camelids). Plain-
# English Part 3: "nothing commissioned", lead time asked 4 September, no answer.
# Asked: the LIS supplier, informally. Not commissioned.
tag: What number do I carry?
luis: 826, 18, twelve digits, once I'm commissioned
tag: Nobody's asked you yet, have they?                   #number

# 7. The alpaca. ASSUMED. A054 CONTESTED (legacy identifiers before 21 April 2021 are
# grandfathered under Art 87(4); date confirmed for deer, GB applicability still queried
# for camelids). 12 Oct: microchip grandfathering parked with policy and legal.
# Asked: lawyers. Not the people holding the animals.
alpaca: I was chipped in 2019. Does that count?
apha_c: For deer, yes. For you, still with the lawyers
alpaca: So a tag in my ear as well?

# ---- Act 4: record keeping ----

# 8. The register. CONTESTED. A077 (digital-only register is a Deputy Director choice, not
# EU law; policy reviewer restates a Day 1 database is required). A076 CONFIRMED for pigs,
# contradicted for camelids. 12 Oct: no holding register exists today, everything is new,
# the minimum is a digital form. Asked: policy. No keeper has seen a template.
keeper2: Do I keep a register?
apha: Yes, from Day 1. All of it is new to you
keeper2: A notebook? A spreadsheet? An app?
apha: Policy wants digital. The law would take paper     #register

# 9. The database. ASSUMED. A099 CONFIRMED as intent (a central camelid database is a
# Day 1 policy requirement), system and owner unresolved between EUDP, LITP and DDTS;
# 12 Oct: LIS as holding register "if LITP has capacity". A103 OPEN (value-for-money risk
# from "planned decommissioning"), but the platform register's own open question of 16 Sep
# is which LIS that means: the company product (EN-14) is being wound down as the Defra
# build (EN-15) takes its functions; the Defra build is in active development. A037 SAM
# frozen until April 2027. Asked: architects. Nobody who would type into it.
register: Who do I report to?
sam: Policy says a central database by Day 1
register: Which one?
samfrozen: Not me, I'm frozen. Maybe LIS, if the new one has room  #database

# ---- Act 5: monitoring ----

# 10. The show. PROPOSED. A071 CONTESTED (no domestic movement reporting is needed at
# Day 1); 12 Oct: no movement-reporting or compliance-check obligation for camelids.
# Plain-English Part 4: camelid movements lawful only from registered holdings, enforced
# by status rather than a system. Asked: policy. Nobody has asked a keeper who shows.
keeper3: Two to a show on Saturday. What do I file?
apha_b: Nothing, if you're registered. If not, it's unlawful
keeper3: How would anyone know?

# ---- The gap, and the hope ----

# 11. The keeper nobody asked. THE UCD GAP. Plain-English Part 3 GOV.UK: guidance has to
# reach camelid keepers outside the farming networks; the dealer landing page needs a
# content owner. No keeper research exists for camelids on this programme; the LIS
# research (five participants, Aug 2026) was cattle. Asked: architects, suppliers,
# policy, lawyers. Not one keeper. The last line is the hope.
keeper3: Has anyone asked how I'd actually do this?
govuk: There's guidance on GOV.UK
keeper3: I'm not in a farming network. Who tells me?        #who-tells-me
apha_c: We've asked architects and suppliers. Not keepers
keeper3: Then ask me. I'd help you design it               #hope
